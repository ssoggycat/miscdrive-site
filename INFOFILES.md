## file names
<p><i>(uncropped)</i> - a lower quality image but with less / no space around it cropped. <br>
<i>(hq)</i> - a notable higher quality version. they're mostly unlabeled</p>

<p>each image has a random self-explanatory name (or an inside joke) assigned for convenience.<br>
despite keeping the exact same data, all of the <i>.jpeg</i> files are renamed to <i>.jpg</i> to be compatible with other services.</p>

## quality
<p>there are so many shark images that new ones sometimes get found in the most bizzare places, or might just not be fully processed in the web archives. that's why the uploads here might have really low resolution. <br>
check google lens / messages on soggy world, there might be higher quality versions just laying out there</p>
